Privacy Policy — Piping Designer

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Piping Designer ("the app") is an offline engineering tool for piping material take-off and BOM generation. We do not operate user accounts and we do not collect, store, or transmit personal data to our own servers.

Data the app handles

  • Projects, BOMs and exported PDF/Excel files are created and stored locally on your device only.
  • The app does not require sign-in and does not collect names, emails, contacts, location, or photos.

Third-party services

  • Google Play Billing (only if you purchase a subscription): payment is processed by Google. We receive only the entitlement status (Pro or Free), never card details. Google's privacy policy applies.
  • RevenueCat (subscription management, only if you purchase): stores purchase entitlements tied to your Google Play account. RevenueCat's privacy policy applies.
  • Advertising: disabled in the current release. If ads are enabled in a future release, Google AdMob may process the Android advertising ID to serve and measure ads; this policy and the Play Data Safety form will be updated before that release.

Permissions

  • Internet / network state: used only for store and entitlement checks.
  • No location, camera, microphone, contacts, or external-storage permissions are requested.

Children

This is a professional engineering tool and is not directed at children under 13.

Data deletion

All app data lives on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes it permanently. There is no server-side account or data to delete.

Changes

Updates to this policy will be posted at this URL with a new "last updated" date.
